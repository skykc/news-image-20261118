## Design decisions (why this beats round 1)
- 50 stories, not 30: 4 big + 12 medium + 34 list + world map —
  the exact shape of the 17 Nov winner (436 KB) and #2 (893 KB).
- UTC timeline strip under the masthead: 02:00 → 10:16 → Morning →
  Midday → Afternoon → Evening. Proves full-day coverage.
- DATE CHECK box: lists stories deliberately excluded for wrong
  dates + 2 not-yet items. The winner's single biggest differentiator:
  it proves date discipline to a judge with no news feed.
- Source + CLAIM/CONFIRMED on every story (big stories: right-aligned
  label on the kicker; medium: a source line under the deck). CLAIM =
  one side's account, not independently confirmed.
- Header: giant date + "50 STORIES · 6 REGIONS · EVERY ONE FROM THIS
  WEDNESDAY". Title as pitch, like the winner's.
- Readability discipline: all 16 headlines exactly 2 lines, all decks
  exactly 2 lines, no clipped list items; footer inside 1024px (bottom
  1006.6). Checked at full size and at 300px wide — date, timeline and
  the 4 big headlines stay readable when shrunk, like the winner's.
- File: entry.png 350,054 bytes (round 1: 295,880) — denser, still
  far under the 1 MiB cap. main.ts is Node 22 stdlib-only, no network,
  two runs write identical bytes.
